**Core value proposition:** Azercell Business is the single connectivity and technology partner that scales with a company — from a single business SIM to enterprise-grade IoT, cloud, and fixed infrastructure — reducing the number of vendors a business has to manage.

## Tariffs & Connectivity
- **[My Business tariff plans](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/mobile-communications/business-tariffs.html)** — *"The corporate advantage."* Nation-wide coverage and predictable billing built around how a company actually operates, not a consumer template.
- **[My Business internet packs](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/mobile-communications/internet.html)** (Monthly / Short-term / Social Networks) — Match data spend to how the business actually uses the internet — from steady monthly use to short bursts to social-only bundles for field teams.
- **[My Business Club](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/mobile-communications/my-business-club.html)** — A loyalty/perks layer for business subscribers — rewards long-term commitment rather than just billing it.
- **[My Business Wi-Fi](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/mobile-communications/biznesim-wi-fi.html)** — *"Keep your business connected better."* Extends connectivity beyond the SIM into the physical workplace.
- **[Roaming (B2B)](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/mobile-communications/roaming.html)** — *"Stay connected anywhere in the world."* Keeps travelling employees productive and reachable without bill-shock.

## Value-Added & Marketing Services
- **[Mobile marketing](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/mobile-communications/special-services/mobile-marketing.html)** — Turns the operator's own channel into a B2B marketing tool for the client's outreach campaigns.
- **[Bulk & Profile SMS](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/mobile-communications/special-services/bulk-sms.html)** — Enterprise-grade messaging infrastructure without the client having to build it themselves.
- **[Content Services](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/mobile-communications/special-services/call-forwarding.html)** — Call-forwarding and related services that keep a business reachable and organized.
- **[Online Itemized Bill](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/mobile-communications/special-services/online-itemized-bill.html)** — Transparency and expense-control tool for finance/procurement teams.
- **[Azercell Biznes (IoT & M2M platform)](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/mobile-communications/special-services/azercell-biznes.html)** — The gateway product tying together all of Azercell's IoT offerings — one dashboard instead of many vendors.

## Fixed & Cloud Infrastructure
- **[Unified service](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/unified-communications.html)** — Positions Azercell as a full communications partner (mobile + fixed + cloud + contact centre), not just a mobile-only vendor.
- **[My Business Internet Leased Line](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/fixed-communications/my-business-internet-leased-line.html)** — Dedicated, reliable connectivity for businesses that can't tolerate consumer-grade internet variability.
- **[Customer Experience Management Platform (CPaaS)](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/cloud-services/cpaas.html)** — Lets businesses build their own customer communications (SMS, voice, chat) on Azercell's infrastructure instead of building from scratch.
- **[InfoHUB](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/cloud-services/infohub.html)** — *"Monitor and optimize."* Turns raw business data into an actionable operations dashboard.

## IoT & Smart Business (the largest B2B product cluster)
- **[YolDASH360](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/iot/yoldash-360.html)** — Smart fleet video-surveillance — safety, accountability, and dispute resolution for fleet operators.
- **[Robotic Automation Solution (RPA)](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/iot/rpa.html)** — Automates repetitive back-office processes — positions Azercell as a productivity partner, not just connectivity.
- **[M2M](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/iot/m2m.html)** — The technical backbone for any connected-device business case — sensors, meters, kiosks, vending, etc.
- **[Mobile device management](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/iot/mobil-cihazlarin-idar-edilmsi.html)** — Enterprise control and security over company-owned devices — IT risk reduction as a service.
- **[Irrigation Control System](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/iot/suvarmaya-nezaret-sistemi.html)** — Remote farm/field monitoring — a concrete agritech vertical use case for M2M.
- **[Mobile team management](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/iot/mobil-komandanin-idare-edilmesi.html)** — Visibility into field workforce location/status — operational efficiency for logistics-heavy businesses.
- **[My Business Fleet](https://azercell.com/en/corporate/iot/biznesim-neqliyyat.html)** — Real-time transport/fleet management — cost control and utilization visibility for fleet-owning businesses.
