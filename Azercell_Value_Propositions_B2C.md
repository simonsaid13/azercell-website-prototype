# Azercell.com — Value Propositions: B2C (Personal)

Reference doc for the interactive prototype / wireframe work on the website revamp. Use these lines as the messaging hook for each card, section, or hero moment in the wireframes. Each item links to its live page on azercell.com (or the relevant external domain).

**Assumption (labeled):** value propositions below are synthesized from the sitemap catalogue and observed site content (Sections 1–5 of `Azercell_Sitemap.docx`), not copy-audited verbatim from every page. Where the current site's own tagline was directly observed, it's quoted; everything else is a concise, source-grounded value statement for prototype/wireframe use — treat as a first draft for copywriting, not final UX copy.

---

**Core value proposition:** Azercell is the everyday connectivity partner for individuals in Azerbaijan — the largest, fastest network, paired with a growing bundle of lifestyle apps and fintech tools that make the SIM more than just a SIM.

## Tariffs & Plans
- **[DigiMax (Prepaid)](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/mobile-tariffs/prepaid/digimax.html)** — *"With your rhythm!"* Flexible prepaid plan across several data/minute tiers (1–30 days) for users who don't want to commit to a fixed monthly contract.
- **[Premium+ Prepaid](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/mobile-tariffs/prepaid/premium-plus-prepaid.html)** — High-volume prepaid (60GB/100GB) with roaming data bundled in, for heavy users who still prefer prepaid control.
- **["Data+" tariff](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/mobile-tariffs/prepaid/data-tarifi.html)** — A data-first prepaid option for subscribers whose primary need is internet, not voice.
- **[Data (prepaid)](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/mobile-tariffs/prepaid/data.html)** — Pure data packs, no voice — for secondary lines, tablets, or backup connectivity.
- **[Alfa (Postpaid)](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/mobile-tariffs/postpaid/alfa.html)** — *"Choices tailored to different needs."* The flagship postpaid family for subscribers who want predictable monthly billing and a plan shaped around their usage profile.
- **[Veteran tariff](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/mobile-tariffs/prepaid/veteran-tarifi.html)** — An exclusive, discounted tariff for war veterans — connectivity as a form of national recognition and CSR.
- **[Əsgərcell](https://azercell.com/en/personal/esgercell.html)** — *"We are at your service with Əsgərcell!"* A dedicated benefits program for active servicemen, reinforcing Azercell's role in national life beyond pure telecom.

## Internet & Data
- **[Internet packs (hub)](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/internet-pack.html)** — One place to match data needs to usage pattern: monthly, weekly, daily, or unlimited.
- **[High-volume / Monthly packs](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/internet-pack/monthly.html)** — For subscribers who want to buy data once and not think about it again.
- **[Weekly packs](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/internet-pack/weekly.html)** — Mid-commitment option for predictable short-term usage (e.g. a work trip, a busy week).
- **[Daily packs](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/internet-pack/daily.html)** — Low-commitment, pay-as-you-go data for light or occasional users.
- **[Unlimited packs](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/internet-pack/unlimited.html)** — Peace of mind for heavy streamers/gamers — no data anxiety.
- **[GiqaAddım campaign](https://azercell.com/en/personal/campaigns/special-offers/giqaaddim-kampaniyasi.html)** — *"Turn your steps into gigabytes!"* Gamifies data top-ups by rewarding physical activity — a differentiator vs. a plain discount promo.

## Roaming & Travel
- **[Roaming (hub)](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/roaming.html)** — *"Stay connected anywhere in the world."* Removes the fear of "going dark" abroad — Azercell travels with you.
- **[Countries and prices](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/roaming/countries-and-prices.html)** — Transparency tool: know exactly what a trip will cost before you leave.
- **[Roaming internet packs](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/roaming/roaming-data-packages.html)** — Pre-buy data for a trip at a predictable price instead of risking pay-per-MB roaming charges.
- **[Travel packs (tourist tariffs)](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/roaming/tourist-tariffs.html)** — A welcome mat for inbound visitors — get connected in Azerbaijan in minutes, no local paperwork.

## Network & Technology
- **[eSIM](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/esim-category.html)** — The modern, physical-SIM-free way to join or add a line — instant activation, ideal for dual-SIM and traveler use cases.
- **[Azercell 5G](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/5g.html)** — Positions Azercell as the technology leader — "the future's close to you" — even where 5G is still in early rollout.
- **[VoLTE](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/volte-category.html)** — Better call quality and faster call setup over the existing 4G network, marketed as a quiet technical upgrade rather than a product.

## Money & Credit
- **[SimCredit](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/services-and-options/0-balance-options/sim-credit.html)** — A safety net when the balance hits zero — never get cut off at the worst moment.
- **[Paycell](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/services-and-options/0-balance-options/paycell.html)** — Turns the mobile account into a lightweight payment tool, blurring the line between telecom and fintech.
- **[akart (Fintech)](https://akart.az)** — Combines banking operations with mobile services under one brand — the boldest expression of "more than a telco."
- **[Aicell](https://azercell.com/en/personal/plans-and-services/services-and-options/aicell.html)** — AI-branded service layer — positions Azercell as an AI-forward operator, not just a connectivity provider.

## Digital Lifestyle Apps (bundled ecosystem)
- **[Azercell Kabinetim (self-service app)](https://azercell.com/en/personal/digital-solutions/azercell-kabinetim.html)** — Full account control in your pocket: balance, tariff switching, subscriptions, top-up — the daily-use hub for every subscriber.
- **[Yandex Plus](https://azercell.com/en/personal/digital-solutions/yandex-plus.html)** — One subscription unlocks Kinopoisk, Yandex Music, and Yandex Books — entertainment bundling that adds daily-use value beyond data.
- **[Busuu](https://azercell.com/en/personal/digital-solutions/busuu.html)** — Turns idle phone time into language learning — self-improvement as a mobile perk.
- **[Litres](https://azercell.com/en/personal/digital-solutions/litres.html)** — Thousands of books/audiobooks in one tap — positions the SIM as a gateway to culture, not just connectivity.
- **[Azercell Kids](https://azercell.com/en/personal/digital-solutions/kids.html)** — Safe, educational content for the youngest family members — extends the value prop to the whole household.
- **[Kinon](https://azercell.com/en/personal/digital-solutions/kinon-plus.html)** — *"Watch the world with us."* 240+ channels across devices — a serious entertainment alternative to a standalone streaming subscription.
- **[Micromobility](https://azercell.com/en/personal/digital-solutions/micromobility.html)** — Pay for scooters/bikes through the mobile account — connectivity extended into urban mobility.
- **[AzParking](https://azercell.com/en/personal/digital-solutions/azparking.html)** — Pay for parking without a separate app or cash — everyday urban-life convenience.
- **[NaviMax](https://azercell.com/en/personal/digital-solutions/navimax.html)** — GPS vehicle tracking via the Azercell ecosystem — practical utility for drivers/parents.
- **[SMSRadar](https://azercell.com/en/personal/digital-solutions/sms-radar.html)** — All-in-one driving utility (fines, alerts) — positions Azercell as part of daily commute life, not just a network.
