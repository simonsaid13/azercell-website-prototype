# Azercell.com — Product & Pricing Database: B2C (Personal)

Working database for the interactive prototype: every product/tariff/service name, price, billing period, and short description available on azercell.com's Personal section, with a source link back to the live page. All prices in AZN and VAT-inclusive unless stated otherwise.

**Assumption (labeled):** where a page lists per-country roaming rates for ~190 countries, or per-country/operator lookups, only the pricing structure plus a handful of representative examples are captured here — not all 190 rows. Full live tables are linked. Prices reflect the site as fetched on 28 July 2026 and are subject to change on the live site.
